Qualifications & Parameters

Focus: Providing Direct Clinical Service

Pay Frequency: 2x per month (on the 11th and 26th of each month).

Description: An individual who is new to the field of Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) and has completed the necessary training and certification requirements to begin providing direct therapy under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA).

  • High school diploma or equivalent (a bachelor’s degree in psychology or a related field is often preferred but not required).
  • Completion of the RBT training program (at least 40 hours of instruction in ABA principles and practices).
  • Successful completion of the RBT competency assessment.
  • Successful completion of the RBT exam through the Behavior Analyst Certification Board (BACB).

Expectations & Restrictions

  • In-Office Requirement: 100% On-Site / In-Clinic / In-home presence required during scheduled client hours.
  • Scheduling: Fixed, set scheduling template approved by the Clinical Director.
  • Duties: Primary focus is direct clinical service.
