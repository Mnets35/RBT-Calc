Qualifications & Parameters

Focus: Providing Direct Clinical Service

Pay Frequency: 2x per month (on the 11th and 26th of each month).

Description: An individual who provides direct therapy under the supervision of a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA).

  • High school diploma or equivalent.
  • Completion of 40-hour RBT training curriculum.
  • Successful completion of RBT competency assessment.
  • Active RBT credential in good standing through the BACB.

Expectations & Restrictions

  • In-Office Requirement: 100% On-Site / In-Clinic / In-home presence required during scheduled client hours.
  • Scheduling: Fixed, set scheduling template approved by the Clinical Director. PTO is accrued based on hours worked and must be applied when requesting off from work.
  • Duties: Primary focus is direct clinical service.

Additional Benefits & Perks

Note: Indirect/non-billable hours are compensated at $15.00/hr. Total package estimates include employer-paid FICA, healthcare contributions upon election, and benefit allowances.
